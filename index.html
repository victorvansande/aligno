Lining up your shot

FadeYour last photo sits see-through on the live view. The slider above the modes makes it stronger or fainter.
EdgesOnly the outlines of your last photo, in pink. Easiest when the scene itself changed a lot.
BlendShows the difference. The darker the screen, the better you’re lined up. Move until it goes almost black.

Handy extras

  • Zoom steps (1×, 2×, ...) give the same zoom every time. The camera opens at the zoom of your last photo.
  • Grid also shows a level, so the phone is straight every time.
  • Timer (top) gives you 3 or 10 seconds, handy for photos of yourself.
  • Photo icon (top) lines up against any picture from your gallery instead.

Photo date

Photos are kept in date order. Fix the date if an imported photo landed in the wrong place.
